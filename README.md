# Impressive Paper List

This list began as a small collection to celebrate the remarkable work of my colleague, Thanawin. Over time, it has taken on a life of its own—growing into a broader compilation of impressive research papers by Thai researchers.

The list is by no means comprehensive, and contributions are warmly welcome!
If you know of any outstanding papers that should be included, please feel free to contact me or open a pull request.

"Searching and Mining Trillions of Time Series Subsequences under Dynamic Time Warping."  (Also Test of Time Award, SIGKDD 2022) [paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2339530.2339576)

2012 EC - best paper award: Paul Dütting, Felix A. Fischer, Petch Pichayut Jirapinyo John K. Lai, Benjamin Lubin, David C. Parkes. "Payment rules through discriminant-based classifiers" [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.1184)
